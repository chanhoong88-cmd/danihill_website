Ceremony & Reception Spaces

Outdoor settings for every part of the day

Guests arrive through the Tunnel of Trees to the Mansion and its Porch. From there the Lawn, the gazebo and the Cloud Garden terraces give you a separate setting for the ceremony, cocktails and dinner.

01 · The Garden Lawn

Vows under open sky and pines

The lawn in front of the Mansion is the main ceremony space, framed by pine trees and the hills. The cool air keeps an afternoon ceremony comfortable for every guest.

02 · The Gazebo & Terrace

For every tradition

Couples have held akad nikah in the gazebo, Hindu ceremonies beneath a mandap on the terrace and garden church-style vows on the lawn. Chinese tea ceremonies can be held inside the Mansion.

03 · Receptions

Dinner on the lawn, under umbrellas or a marquee

Set round tables on the lawn, long banquet tables in the pavilion or a clear marquee for up to 300 guests. If it rains, marquee tents and canopies keep the garden plan going under cover.

Stays for Guests

Sleep where you celebrate

The heritage Mansion and Villa Dani each sleep up to 15, so 30 of your closest family and friends can stay on the estate. Every package includes a bridal room for getting ready.

Book the 3-day, 2-night package to have both houses for the whole wedding weekend. Other guests can stay at hotels in Genting Highlands, a short drive away.

FAQ

Wedding questions

How much does a wedding at Dani Hill cost?

Venue rental starts at RM9,000 for a weekday half-day. A full day is RM15,000 on weekdays and RM18,000 on weekends. The 3-day, 2-night package with both houses is RM23,000 on weekdays and RM28,000 on weekends. Prices are for the venue only.

How many guests can Dani Hill host?

Up to 300 guests as standard, and 301 to 500 with a RM3,000 surcharge. We host one wedding per day.

Can we bring our own caterer, decorator and planner?

Yes. Dani Hill is venue-only, so you work with your own team. We're happy to recommend caterers and event organisers we've worked with.

Where can our guests stay?

The Mansion and Villa Dani each sleep up to 15, so up to 30 guests can stay on site. Other guests can stay at hotels in Genting Highlands, a short drive away.

What happens if it rains?

The highlands are typically 18–22°C, with mist in the mornings and evenings. The wet-weather plan is marquee tents and canopies in the garden, so the day stays outdoors under cover.

How far is Dani Hill from Kuala Lumpur?

About 45 minutes by car. The estate is in Gohtong Jaya, Genting Highlands, and 40-seater coaches can drive up to it.

Can we visit before we book?

Yes. Site visits are by appointment. Request a viewing or message us on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a time.
