Privacy notice

This notice explains how Dani Hill, operated by Accel Hospitality Management Sdn. Bhd. (202601033619), uses the information you share through this website. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

  • The details you enter in our enquiry and viewing forms, such as your name, phone number, email, preferred dates, guest numbers and any message.
  • How you found us, such as the advertising campaign or website that sent you here. We keep this with your enquiry so we know which channels work.
  • If you accept cookies, anonymous information about how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and buttons clicked.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange viewings and prepare proposals and bookings.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns are useful, and to improve the website and our advertising.

We do not send your name, phone number, email or message to analytics or advertising tools, and we do not sell your information.

Cookies

Analytics and advertising cookies are only used if you choose Accept in the cookie banner. If you choose Decline, the site still works. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Keeping and accessing your information

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to handle your enquiry or booking and to meet our legal obligations. You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, by contacting us on 011-6226 5257.

We handle personal data in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
