A Chinese tea ceremony in a heritage mansion

By Danihill Media · 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Chinese Wedding Tea Ceremony and Garden Reception in Genting Highlands

A Chinese wedding day in Malaysia often has several parts: the groom's arrival and the gate crash, the tea ceremony with both families, and a dinner in the evening. Most couples split these between the family home and a restaurant or hotel. On a private estate you can keep them in one place, and give each its own setting.

The tea ceremony

The tea ceremony is about respect for elders, so it needs a calm, comfortable room where parents and grandparents can sit while the couple serve tea in order of seniority. At Dani Hill, Chinese tea ceremonies can be held inside the Mansion, a heritage house restored in 2026 with a grand staircase, chandeliers and period furniture. It gives the ceremony a formal setting without needing a backdrop.

Agree the order of serving and the list of relatives with both families in advance, and appoint someone to call names. Your photographer will want to know where the couple will kneel or stand and where the elders will sit.

The bridal suite and the gate crash

Getting ready in the bridal room gives the bride's party space for hair, make-up and photographs, and gives the groom's party somewhere to aim for. Every Dani Hill package includes a bridal room. Plan the gate crash games with the bridesmaids beforehand and give them a time limit, so the day stays on schedule.

Dinner in the garden

Instead of a ten-course banquet in a ballroom, many couples now host the dinner on the lawn or under a clear marquee, with long tables and lights strung overhead. Dani Hill is venue-only, so you bring your own caterer, whether that is a Chinese banquet caterer or a Western menu. We can recommend caterers we have worked with.

The highland air helps here. With evenings typically in the high teens, guests are comfortable outdoors, and some will want a light jacket. Mention it on the invitation.

Auspicious dates

Many families consult the Chinese almanac or a fortune teller for the wedding date. Popular dates book out early, so check availability as soon as you have two or three options. Dani Hill hosts one wedding per day, so a date is yours once confirmed.

Guests and stays

Up to 300 guests can attend as standard, and up to 500 with a surcharge. The Mansion and Villa Dani sleep up to 30, which suits both families staying the night before for an early start. The estate is about 45 minutes from Kuala Lumpur, and 40-seater coaches can drive up.
