A highland wedding for Singapore couples

By Danihill Media · 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Singapore Couples Marrying in Malaysia: A Guide to a Genting Highlands Wedding

For Singapore couples, a wedding in Malaysia offers what is hard to find at home: a private estate with gardens, cool weather and room for family to stay. Genting Highlands is a short flight or a long drive away, and the cost of a full wedding weekend can compare well with a ballroom in Singapore.

Why the highlands

Dani Hill sits in Gohtong Jaya, Genting Highlands, where temperatures typically stay between 18 and 22°C. A garden ceremony at midday is comfortable, the pines and hills frame every photograph, and the evenings are cool enough for a jacket. We host one wedding per day, so the estate is yours.

Getting there

Most guests fly into Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) and travel up by car or coach. The estate is about 45 minutes from the city, and 40-seater coaches can drive up to it. Others drive from Singapore through Johor and make a road trip of it. Private chauffeur transfers can be arranged for the couple and close family.

The legal side

Many couples marry legally at home and hold the celebration in Malaysia, which keeps the paperwork simple. If you want the legal solemnisation to take place in Malaysia, check the requirements with the relevant authorities in both countries well in advance. A religious ceremony, such as an akad nikah or a church blessing, will also have its own requirements.

Vendors from home or local

Dani Hill is venue-only, so you can bring a planner, photographer or decorator from Singapore, or work with Malaysian vendors. Many couples combine the two: a planner or photographer from home, with a local caterer and decorator who know the estate. We can introduce vendors we have worked with.

Make it a weekend

A destination wedding works best over two or three days. The three-day, two-night package includes both houses, the Mansion and Villa Dani, for up to 30 guests. Host a welcome dinner on the first evening, the wedding on the second day and a slow breakfast before everyone heads home. The wider guest list can stay at hotels in Genting Highlands, a short drive away.

Budgeting in ringgit

Venue rental starts at RM9,000 for a weekday half-day, and the full wedding weekend package is RM23,000 on weekdays and RM28,000 on weekends. Prices are for the venue only. See the full price guide, and our guide to destination weddings.

Frequently asked questions

How do guests from Singapore get to Dani Hill?

Most fly into KLIA and travel up by car or coach, about 45 minutes from Kuala Lumpur. Others drive up through Johor. 40-seater coaches can reach the estate.

Can we bring our Singapore wedding planner?

Yes. Dani Hill is venue-only, so you can bring vendors from Singapore or work with Malaysian vendors we recommend.

Where can our guests stay?

Up to 30 guests can stay on site in the Mansion and Villa Dani. Others can stay at hotels in Genting Highlands.

Can we get legally married in Malaysia?

Requirements differ by country and religion. Many couples marry legally at home and celebrate at Dani Hill. Check with the relevant authorities early.
