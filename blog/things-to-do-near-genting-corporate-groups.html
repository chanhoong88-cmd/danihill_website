Meet in the morning, get out in the afternoon

By Danihill Media · 13 November 2026 · 2 min read

Things to Do Near Genting Highlands for Corporate Groups

A good company retreat needs two things: a quiet place to think, and something to do once the thinking is done. Hold the meeting in a heritage house, then spend the afternoon out in the highlands. Here is what a corporate group can do at and around Dani Hill.

Start with the work

Dani Hill is about 45 minutes from KL: close enough for a day trip, far enough that the office feels a long way off. The Mansion works as a meeting house for teams of up to 30 staying over, with a meeting TV, a whiteboard and a PA system, plus breakout areas for smaller groups. For company celebrations and day events, the estate takes up to 300 guests, or up to 500 with a surcharge.

Then get out: four ideas nearby

  1. Golf at Awana. The Awana golf course is right beside the estate, ideal for a leadership group or a client day.
  2. Eufloria Gardens. An easy walk through flower gardens, good for teams who want fresh air without a big effort.
  3. Genting Highlands Premium Outlets. A popular stop on the way home, especially for out-of-town teams.
  4. ATV rides. For teams who want something with more energy, an off-road ride makes a good shared story.

Or stay in

Some of the best team moments happen back at the house.

  • An activity room with an automatic mahjong table, cards and board games, pool, darts and foosball.
  • Karaoke and movie night for the evening crowd.
  • A charcoal BBQ pit for a relaxed team dinner.
  • The private Chef's Table in the Mansion for a sit-down dinner.
  • Spa treatments on request, and a quiet night in Villa Dani.

Getting the team there

40-seater coaches can drive up to the estate, so the whole team can arrive together. For smaller leadership groups, a private chauffeur can be arranged.

A sample one-night retreat

  • Day 1, morning: coach up from KL, strategy session in the Mansion.
  • Day 1, afternoon: golf, gardens or ATV rides.
  • Day 1, evening: BBQ dinner, then mahjong, karaoke or a movie.
  • Day 2: breakfast, a short wrap-up session, and a stop at the outlets on the way home.

For on-site activity ideas, see team building ideas for groups of 10 to 30. For formats and facilities, see the corporate retreats page, or contact us for a proposal.

Frequently asked questions

What is there to do near Dani Hill?

Golf at Awana beside the estate, Eufloria Gardens, Genting Highlands Premium Outlets and ATV rides, plus an activity room, karaoke and a BBQ pit on the estate.

Can we hold meetings on site?

Yes. The Mansion has a meeting TV, a whiteboard and a PA system, with breakout areas, for teams of up to 30 staying over.

How big a group can you host?

Up to 30 can stay overnight. Day events can host up to 300 guests, or up to 500 with a surcharge.

Can a coach reach the estate?

Yes. 40-seater coaches can drive up to the estate.
