Golf by day, a private house by night

By Danihill Media · 13 November 2026 · 2 min read

A Golf Weekend at Awana, Genting Highlands, With a Private House for the Group

Most golf trips end the same way. A good round, then everyone splits up into separate hotel rooms, and the best part of the day, the talking afterwards, gets cut short. At Dani Hill, the Awana golf course is right beside the estate. Play by day, then come back to a private house where the whole group stays together.

Play in cool highland air

Golf in Malaysia usually means an early tee time to beat the heat. Up in Genting Highlands the air typically sits between 18 and 22°C, so a round stays comfortable well past breakfast. The estate is about 45 minutes from KL, so you can leave the city in the morning and be on the course soon after.

Stay together, up to 30 guests

Dani Hill has two houses, the Mansion and Villa Dani. Each sleeps up to 15, so a group of up to 30 can stay on the estate together. Take one house for a smaller group, or both for a bigger one.

  • A personal host who looks after the plan for the weekend.
  • Private housekeeping, so the house stays in order while you are out.
  • A private chauffeur for getting to and from the course.

A sample weekend

  1. Saturday morning: arrive, drop the bags and head to the course.
  2. Saturday afternoon: back at the house for a rest, spa treatments on request, or a game in the activity room: pool, darts, foosball, mahjong and cards.
  3. Saturday evening: dinner at the private Chef's Table in the Mansion, or a charcoal BBQ at the pit. Karaoke for those with the energy.
  4. Sunday: a slow breakfast, a second round for the keen ones, and an easy drive home.

Good for friends, family and clients

  • Friends marking a birthday or a reunion.
  • Families where some play and some don't, with plenty for the non-golfers to do.
  • Companies hosting clients or rewarding a team, with a meeting set-up in the Mansion for a morning session if needed.

Larger groups can come up by coach: 40-seater coaches can drive up to the estate.

Plan your golf weekend

Tell us your dates and the size of your group, and we will put the weekend together. See the residences for both houses, or the corporate retreats page if you are hosting clients.

Frequently asked questions

How far is the golf course from Dani Hill?

The Awana golf course is right beside the estate in Genting Highlands, so the drive to the first tee is short. A private chauffeur can be arranged.

How many people can stay?

Up to 30 across the Mansion and Villa Dani, 15 in each house. You can take one house or both.

What is there for non-golfers?

An activity room with an automatic mahjong table, karaoke, pool, darts and foosball, plus spa treatments on request and gardens to walk.

Can a coach reach the estate?

Yes. 40-seater coaches can drive up to the estate.
