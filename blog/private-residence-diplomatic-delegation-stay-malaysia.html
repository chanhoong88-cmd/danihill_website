Private Residences for Diplomatic Delegations and Official Visits Near Kuala Lumpur

When a delegation visits Malaysia, or an embassy hosts guests from home, a city hotel is the default. It is not always the best choice. A private residence offers what a hotel cannot: exclusive use, discretion, and a setting that makes a quiet conversation possible.

What protocol teams look for

  • Exclusive use: no other guests on the property.
  • Controlled access: one entrance, a known guest list, and grounds that are not open to the public.
  • Proximity: close enough to Kuala Lumpur for meetings, far enough for privacy.
  • Dignified rooms: suites for principals, rooms for staff, and spaces to meet and dine.
  • Flexibility: the ability to adjust timings, menus and arrangements at short notice.

Dani Hill as a private residence

Dani Hill is a private estate in Gohtong Jaya, Genting Highlands, about 45 minutes from Kuala Lumpur. It has two hosted residences. The Mansion is a heritage house restored in 2026, with a grand staircase, chandeliers, formal salons and a dining room. Villa Dani is a more relaxed house with a cinema room and games. Each sleeps up to 15, so up to 30 guests can stay on the estate.

Both houses can be reserved together for exclusive use. The estate is reached through the Tunnel of Trees and is not open to the public.

Hosted dinners

A private dinner can be arranged for your guests on request, served in the Mansion. For larger receptions, the lawn and gardens can host up to 300 guests, with marquee tents and canopies as cover.

Arrival and transport

Private chauffeur transfers can be arranged from Kuala Lumpur or KLIA. Coaches of up to 40 seats can reach the estate for staff and larger groups.

Planning with your security and protocol team

Every official visit has its own requirements. We are happy to arrange an advance site visit for your protocol and security officers, so they can review access, rooms and timings before confirming. Tell us what your team needs, and we will tell you plainly what the estate can and cannot provide.

Suited to

  • Visiting ministers and delegations seeking a private base outside the city.
  • Embassy and high commission retreats and planning days.
  • Hosted dinners for visiting guests.
  • Stays for ambassadors and their families.

Frequently asked questions

Can the whole estate be reserved for a delegation?

Yes. The Mansion and Villa Dani can be reserved together for exclusive use, for up to 30 overnight guests.

How far is Dani Hill from Kuala Lumpur?

About 45 minutes by car. Private chauffeur transfers can be arranged.

Can our security team visit in advance?

Yes. Advance site visits can be arranged by appointment for protocol and security officers.

Can you host a dinner for official guests?

A private dinner can be arranged on request in the Mansion, and the gardens can host receptions of up to 300 guests.
