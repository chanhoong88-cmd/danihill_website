What a venue-only wedding really costs

By Danihill Media · 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

How Much Does a Wedding Venue Cost in Malaysia? A Guide to Venue-Only Pricing

Comparing wedding venues in Malaysia is harder than it should be, because they are priced in different ways. A hotel quotes per table with food included. A garden estate often quotes for the venue alone. Neither is cheaper by default. What matters is what is included and what you will add.

Per-table packages

Hotels and restaurants usually sell a package per table of ten. It bundles the room, the food, basic decoration and service staff. It is simple to budget for, but you are limited to their menu, their decorators or a list of approved vendors, and a ballroom time slot that may be shared with another event that day.

Venue-only

A venue-only estate rents you the space for a set time, and you bring the caterer, decorator, furniture and planner. You have more choice and usually more privacy, but more to coordinate. Dani Hill is venue-only and hosts one wedding a day, so the gardens, the Mansion and Villa Dani are reserved for you.

What Dani Hill costs

As a guide, a weekday half-day starts at RM9,000. A full day from 7am to 11pm is RM15,000 on weekdays and RM18,000 on weekends and public holidays. The three-day, two-night package, which includes both houses for up to 30 guests, is RM23,000 on weekdays and RM28,000 on weekends. Every package includes a bridal room. The full list of add-ons and terms is on the price guide.

What to budget for on top

  • Catering: usually the largest line. Ask for a per-head price that includes service staff, crockery and setup.
  • Furniture and tableware: chairs, tables, linens and lighting, often supplied by the decorator.
  • Decoration: the ceremony arch, florals and the reception styling.
  • Cover: a marquee or canopies for the wet-weather plan, which every outdoor wedding in Malaysia needs.
  • Transport: coaches for guests if the venue is outside the city.
  • Planner or coordinator: worth it for venue-only weddings, to run the day.

Questions to ask before you pay a deposit

  • What hours are included, and does setup and teardown have to fit inside them?
  • Is the venue exclusive on the day, or shared with other events?
  • What is the deposit, when is the balance due, and is the deposit refundable?
  • Is there a security deposit, and what is it held against?
  • Are there rules on cooking, open flames or noise?

At Dani Hill, a 50% deposit confirms your date and the balance is due 30 days before the wedding. A refundable RM3,000 security deposit applies, and setup and teardown must fit within your package hours.

How to compare fairly

Build a like-for-like total. Add your chosen caterer, decorator and cover to the venue-only fee, then compare it with a hotel package for the same number of guests. Then weigh what the money buys: exclusivity, setting, time, and whether your family can stay the night.
