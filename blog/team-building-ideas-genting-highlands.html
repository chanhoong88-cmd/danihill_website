Team building ideas for small groups in the highlands

By Danihill Media · 12 November 2026 · 3 min read

Team Building Ideas in Genting Highlands for Groups of 10 to 30

The best team building does not feel like team building. It gives people a reason to talk to colleagues they rarely work with, a little friendly competition, and a shared story to bring back to the office. For groups of 10 to 30, a private estate gives you the space to do that without sharing it with strangers.

Outdoors, in cool air

Genting Highlands typically sits between 18 and 22°C, so outdoor activities are comfortable even at midday. On the lawn and terraces at Dani Hill you can run:

  • A garden challenge: teams solve a series of puzzles or tasks around the estate, with an event organiser running the clock.
  • A BBQ cook-off: teams plan, cook and present a dish at the charcoal BBQ pit, judged by the leadership team.
  • A photo hunt: a list of shots to capture around the gardens, the Mansion and the Cloud Garden terraces.

Indoors, after dark

The evening is often where teams bond most. The activity room at Dani Hill has an automatic mahjong table, karaoke, pool, darts and foosball, plus cards and board games. A movie night is easy to set up too.

  • A tournament night: pool, darts and foosball brackets, with mixed teams across departments.
  • Karaoke: no explanation needed.
  • A mahjong table: a favourite for many Malaysian teams, and a good leveller between juniors and seniors.

Something quieter

Not everyone wants competition. Build in a slower option: a walk through the Tunnel of Trees, a tea break overlooking the hills, or spa treatments on request. A retreat that respects introverts gets more out of everyone.

Making it work

  • Mix teams across departments and seniority.
  • Keep each activity under 90 minutes.
  • Have a clear winner and a small prize. It matters more than you think.
  • Bring in an event organiser for anything structured. Dani Hill can recommend ones we have worked with.

Staying the night

Up to 30 people can stay across the Mansion and Villa Dani, which turns a day of activities into a proper retreat. The estate is about 45 minutes from KL, and 40-seater coaches can drive up.

See the corporate retreats page for formats and facilities, or contact us for a proposal.

Frequently asked questions

What activities are available on site?

An activity room with an automatic mahjong table, karaoke, pool, darts, foosball, cards and board games, plus movie night and a charcoal BBQ pit. Spa treatments are available on request.

Can you organise the team-building programme?

Dani Hill is venue-only. You can bring your own event organiser, or we can recommend ones we have worked with.

How big a group can you host?

Up to 30 can stay overnight. Day events can host larger groups.

Can a coach reach the estate?

Yes. 40-seater coaches can drive up to the estate.
