FAQ

Corporate questions

How many people can you host?

Day events and company celebrations for up to 300 guests, and up to 500 with a surcharge. Up to 30 people can stay overnight across the Mansion and Villa Dani.

What meeting equipment is on site?

A meeting TV, whiteboard and PA system, with breakout areas around the estate. It suits team meetings of up to 30 on a stay package.

Can you arrange catering and team-building activities?

Dani Hill is venue-only, so you can bring your own caterer and event organiser, or we can recommend ones we've worked with. Private dinners can be arranged on request.

Can a coach reach the estate?

Yes. 40-seater coaches drive up to the estate, about 45 minutes from Kuala Lumpur.

What if it rains?

Meetings and games carry on indoors, and marquee tents and canopies cover garden activities and dinners.

Can we see the estate first?

Yes. Site visits are by appointment. Request a viewing or message us on WhatsApp.
